## 8 Risks and surprises

- **The funnel is stronger than Lanchester**: chasing columns put only
  their front rank in weapon range (measured: five riflemen on one turret
  land about one rifleman's worth of damage; the turret beats a 300-ore
  rifle press at every stat line swept). This is the engine's real a-move
  physics, not a bug — the battery's laws are stated through it, and
  spread formations (a client-side presentation concern) are the A14
  sessions' question. A stat-only fix was considered and rejected: no
  turret line in the swept space (275–350 hp × 8–10 dmg) lets a rifle
  column take it without making the turret useless against the raiders
  and workers it exists to stop.
- **The basin match is 74% longer** (11956 → 20834 at seed 7) — the
  tougher line slows the scripted armies' mutual attrition. The soak
  sample says crossroads pacing barely moved (avg 13869 across 16 seeds
  vs B-030's 14337 — within seed noise), so the effect is map-shaped
  (the basin's three lanes stretch the trades). The nightly's 36000-tick
  budget holds a 42% margin over the observed max (21036).
- The initial design iteration (lancer at 100 hp / 60 dmg / 200 ore) was
  measured and discarded: at that price the piercer loses its own
  matchup 4v4 while the mixed-arms margin collapses; the shipped config
  keeps the lancer at 160 and lets the count edge carry the law.
