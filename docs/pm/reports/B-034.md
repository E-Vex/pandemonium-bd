## 1 Summary

The owner's standing delegation ("lets keep working on upgrading this game...
make sure there is no errors and the ci is green then make a PR. I want to make
a game that can beat Generals Zero Hour") named Update 7 as the next step
through the AI-Handoff's §8 pointer and B-033's report: **the tech tree /
upgrades — the permanent axis of progression**. **ADR-0203** (proposed, the
ADR-0201 flow, A-238) is the design record. One capability (`Upgrades`,
authored spec lists — parameters, prerequisite edges, and target-kind lists,
all resolved to ids at the world seam), one verb (`Research` — researcher,
slot) through the unchanged gate with three new reject reasons and seven
checks (once per PLAYER, the per-building project slot, the player-wide
duplicate-in-flight refusal — the wasted-purse law), the stage-3 research
pipeline (the production head's exact semantics: spend at enqueue, start on
the first advancing tick, complete at the authored tick count), and the
**researched ledger** — one sorted per-player store of completed upgrades
with effect snapshots, permanent and player-wide, read live by combat
(`effective_damage`) and movement (`effective_speed`, upgrade composed
before boost) so nothing mutates at completion and no spawn hook exists. Two
shipped upgrades, both on the Forge — **Weapon Systems** (+25% attack damage
for Rifleman and Raider, 250 ore, 20 s) and **Field Engines** (+15% move
speed for Worker and Pathfinder, 200 ore, 15 s, chained on Weapon Systems —
the tree's first edge). The client's research card row submits directly (the
Train rule) with the in-flight sweep and the `[done]` state.

## 3 Evidence per acceptance criterion

- **The ADR** — `docs/adr/0203-upgrade-system.md` (proposed, every TEMPLATE
  section filled, the frozen decisions named with their exact extension
  shape, evidence cited at the pinned head `3121fba`, alternatives
  considered, the Decision field open for the owner's verdict — A-238).

- **The gate rejects every illegal research** —
  `tests/upgrades.rs::the_research_gate_matrix_rejects_every_illegal_form`:
  unknown researcher (`UnknownEntity`), another player's researcher
  (`NotOwnedByIssuer`), a non-researcher (`MissingCapability`), a slot the
  kind does not have (`UpgradeSlotInvalid`), an unheld prerequisite
  (`RequirementsUnmet`), the busy building (`ResearchBusy`), the same
  upgrade in flight at a second building (`ResearchBusy` — the
  player-wide rule), a re-offered completed upgrade
  (`UpgradeAlreadyResearched`), and an over-priced project against a thin
  purse (`CannotAfford`); each refusal changes no state (the purse and the
  research list are asserted untouched).

- **The tree's edges** — `prerequisites_gate_the_tree_until_the_prerequisite_
  lands`: Field Engines refuses until Weapon Systems lands, then researches
  and completes; both ledger entries present, both costs spent exactly
  (1000 − 40 − 50 = 910).

- **The economy seam and the exact ticks** —
  `research_spends_at_enqueue_and_fires_on_the_exact_ticks`: the cost leaves
  at the tick boundary (the Train rule), `ResearchStarted` fires on the
  first advancing tick with progress 1/30 = 33 thousandths, the block still
  runs at tick 28, and `ResearchCompleted` + the ledger entry land at tick
  29 (the 30th advance) with the block removed.

- **The effects, through the real pipelines** —
  `a_completed_weapon_upgrade_multiplies_the_attack` (the authored 8-damage
  hit lands first; after "guns" completes, the next hit deals 12 — 8 × 1.5
  exactly, through `effective_damage` in the combat pipeline) and
  `a_completed_speed_upgrade_marches_farther` (the same 60-tick march order
  in two identical worlds: the upgraded march outruns the plain one by

  `the_integer_multiplier_law_floors_toward_zero` pins the documented
  rounding (8 × 1.25 = 10; 8 × 1.2 floors at 9).

- **Parallel research and the wasted purse** —
  `two_buildings_research_different_projects_in_parallel`: a second lab

  keeps duplicates refused; both upgrades complete.

- **The death semantics** — `a_dead_researcher_loses_only_the_in_flight_
  project`: a commanded one-shot kill mid-project drops the block with the
  entity and the ledger stays empty; the same kill after completion leaves

  building's).

## 8 Risks and surprises

- The mask's 16 bits are now exhausted (Health through Research): the next
  capability needs a wider mask word, which IS a value shift and a version
  bump — recorded in ADR-0203 §Consequences so the future brief cites it
  rather than discovering it.
- The player-wide duplicate-in-flight refusal (the wasted-purse law) is a
  gate semantics choice beyond the literal Zero-Hour per-building rule —
  it makes the client's card approximation exactly match the gate; the
  alternative (silently dropping the second completion) was rejected in
  the ADR's design discussion.
- The test world's tick discipline (commands must target the live tick —
  no future buffering) bit three test drafts before the suite settled;
  the acceptance suite now pins the exact-tick semantics instead of
  approximating them.
- The sandbox host (2 cores, no GPU, 4 GB) built and ran everything
  through the headless path (ALSA satisfied from a local extraction with
  a doctored pkg-config path — the CI runners install libasound2-dev
  properly); the windowed client's visual truth (the research row's look,
  the sweep's feel) is unverified by human eyes — §11.
