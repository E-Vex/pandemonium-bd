### 8. Risks and surprises

- **The bind-scan test's heuristic is position-sensitive**: it cuts its
  subject file at the first `#[cfg(test)]` — a `cfg(test)` import placed at
  the top of `render/mod.rs` would have silently blinded it. The final
  layout keeps that test's subject (the `Renderer` frame pass) in
  `render/mod.rs` with no top-level cfg(test) lines; its scanned surface
  never moved. Worth remembering for future render/ work.
- **`use super::*` transitivity through private imports** is load-bearing in
  `render/pipeline/mod.rs` (the constructor reaches terrain/entity names
  through the parent's scope). A future name added to a sibling module must
  be re-imported into `render/mod.rs`'s scope (or pipeline's explicit list)
  or the constructor stops resolving — the compiler catches it immediately.
- **The no-WM focus gotcha** (AI-Handoff §9) bit once: XTEST keys go nowhere
