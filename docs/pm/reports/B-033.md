## 1 Summary

The owner's standing delegation ("lets keep working on upgrading this game...
make sure there is no errors and the ci is green then make a PR. I want to make
a game that can beat Generals Zero Hour") named Update 6 and its ADR as the
next step: **the ability system, the first designed extension** — plan §17's
own audit of the command architecture ("Abilities — command machinery,
validation gate, cooldown-ready pipeline"). **ADR-0202** (proposed, the
ADR-0201 flow, A-233) is the design record. One capability (`Abilities`,
multi-slot authored specs — parameters only, the effect vocabulary is code),
one verb (`CastAbility` — caster, slot, ground point) through the unchanged
gate with three new reject reasons and a ground-point fog law (the spotting
seam: a caster cannot shell what it cannot see, a scout's eyes lift it), the
stage-7 cooldown-ready pipeline (the attack-cooldown semantics), the injected
`SpeedBoost` timed modifier (the Construction pattern; movement reads
`effective_speed`), the replay tag, the `AbilityCast` event + `AbilityView`,
and the client's card row with a cooldown sweep and armed-click targeting.
Two shipped abilities, both forge-tier — the Pathfinder's **Overland Sprint**
and the Ballista's **Shatter Volley** (cast range one tile beyond its own
sight, so the pairing the content always promised — the scout spots, the
engine shells — is now the play).

## 3 Evidence per acceptance criterion

- **The ADR** — `docs/adr/0202-ability-system.md` (proposed, every TEMPLATE
  section filled, the frozen decisions named with their exact extension

- **The gate rejects every illegal cast** — `tests/abilities.rs::
  the_cast_gate_matrix_rejects_every_illegal_form`: unknown caster
  (`UnknownEntity`), another player's caster (`NotOwnedByIssuer`), a
  non-caster (`MissingCapability`), a slot the kind does not have
  (`AbilitySlotInvalid`), a point beyond the cast range
  (`AbilityOutOfRange`); each refusal changes no state (the victim stands at
  full hp). `a_blind_cast_refuses_until_a_scout_lifts_the_fog`: a point

  and the spotting seam, both executable.

- **The cooldown-ready pipeline** — `a_ready_slot_blocks_on_cooldown_and_
  recharges_exactly`: the cast blocks the slot the next tick, the slot is
  ready again at exactly `cast_tick + cooldown_ticks` (30 ticks for the
  1000 ms fixture authoring — the attack-cooldown semantics, mirrored).

- **The effects** — `area_damage_kills_enemies_only_and_fires_the_death_
  path` (the 45-damage blast kills the 40-hp victim in the cast's tick,
  `Died` fires, the hit rides `AttackHit` with the caster as attacker;
  the in-blast friendly and the far survivor are untouched),
  `heal_area_restores_clamped_at_max` (60 + 30 clamped under the ongoing
  bleed — 880 milli, the exact integer arithmetic),
  `a_speed_burst_marches_farther_and_expires` (30 boosted ticks at 2×
