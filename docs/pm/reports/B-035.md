## 1 Summary

The owner's standing delegation ("lets keep working on upgrading this game...
make sure there is no errors and the ci is green then make a PR. I want to make
a game that can beat Generals Zero Hour") named Update 8 as the next step
through the AI-Handoff's §4 pointer, ADR-0203's Consequences and B-034's
report §9: **the evaluative AI above the command interface** — the second
brain that uses the verbs Updates 6 and 7 added. **ADR-0204** (proposed, the
ADR-0201 flow, A-242..A-245) is the design record. The `TacticalController`
(crates/ai/src/evaluative.rs) implements the **composition law**: it owns a
`ScriptedController` and forwards every think to it, then runs its two
evaluative systems after — research (raise the first buildable `Upgrades`
carrier with requirements read ahead through the view and the bank rule, then
first-legal-slot research on cooldown, sight-verified through the view's own
`research`/`researched` lists) and casting (per-(caster, slot) directives:
when the slot's readout is ready, cast at the aim cluster's centroid — the
densest sighted enemy grid-bucket for DamageArea, the weakest own bucket for
HealArea, the push-nearest own bucket for SpeedBurst — clamped to the authored
range with exact integer math). The engine gains `eval_plan`/`eval_controller`
(the alpha twins' signature, capability-shaped derivation, never
name-matched); the tools gain `--p1 tactical`/`--p2 tactical`; the recorded
`ControllerKind` stays `Ai` (A-060). **Zero content edits, zero
sim/sim_api/replay/client changes, no registered golden moves** — the first
§19 update with none.
