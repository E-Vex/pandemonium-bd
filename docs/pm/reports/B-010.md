  B-002: rotate it after merge (§11).
- **The victory-parameters gap is real and now on the record.** The rules'
  `victory` block is validated and hashed today, but stage 10 hard-codes
  elimination and applies `Resign` unconditionally of `allow_resignation`
  (ADR §1's citations). Harmless while "elimination" is the only kind and
  resignation is always allowed — but the first mission brief must close it by
  making stage 10 actually data-driven, or mission objectives inherit a
  half-wired precedent. The ADR says so in §1 and §2 rather than silently
  fixing it.

  command gate from faction production lists; buildability is only
  roster-derived UI today — a restricted roster hides the button but does not
  forbid the command. Flagged in ADR §3 with the named fix (a Build-gate
  membership check) so mission restrictions are authoritative, not cosmetic,
  when they land.
- **AI defaults are load-bearing.** Any touch to the scripted controller's
  consts or its RNG consumption order moves the flagship golden; ADR §4
  carries the four proof obligations and the standing 32-seed sweep warning
  forward for the implementing brief.
- **CI runs the full gate even on docs-only branches** (B-007/B-008's note,
